Welding Safety

Welding can expose workers to intense light, heat, fumes, electrical hazards, and fire risks. Follow your training and workplace safety procedures every time you weld.

Wear Proper Protective Equipment

Use a correctly rated welding helmet, safety glasses, welding gloves, flame-resistant clothing, and sturdy footwear. Make sure protective equipment is in good condition and appropriate for the process.

Protect Your Work Area

Keep combustible materials away from sparks and hot metal. Use screens to protect nearby people from arc flash, and keep suitable fire-extinguishing equipment accessible.

Use Safe Ventilation

Work in a well-ventilated area and use appropriate fume extraction. Follow safety guidance for the metals, coatings, and consumables involved.

Inspect Equipment

Check cables, connections, hoses, and tools before use. Do not use damaged equipment; report problems and follow your shop's lockout and maintenance procedures.
