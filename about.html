About Welding

Welding is the process of joining two or more pieces of metal using heat, pressure, or both. It is an important skill used in construction, manufacturing, automotive work, and many other industries.

I am interested in welding because it combines hands-on work, creativity, problem-solving, and attention to detail. I enjoy learning how metal can be shaped and joined to create useful and durable products.

Types of Welding

MIG Welding

MIG welding uses a continuously fed wire electrode and shielding gas to join metal. It is commonly used in fabrication and automotive projects.

TIG Welding

TIG welding uses a non-consumable tungsten electrode. It provides precise control and is useful for detailed welding work.

Stick Welding

Stick welding uses a coated electrode to create an electrical arc that joins metal. It is often used in construction and repair work.

Welding Safety

Safety is one of the most important parts of welding. Proper protective equipment and safe working conditions help prevent injuries.

  • Wear an appropriate welding helmet.
  • Use welding gloves and protective clothing.
  • Work in a properly ventilated area.
  • Keep flammable materials away from sparks.
  • Follow equipment safety instructions.
